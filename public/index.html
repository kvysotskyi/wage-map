Wage level, or your salary

How the H-1B lottery estimate works

Since the FY 2027 cap season, USCIS enters each H-1B registration into the lottery once to four times, depending on the highest OEWS wage level the offered salary meets for that occupation and work location: Level I one entry, II two, III three, IV four. The draw is still random. A salary below Level I is under the prevailing wage and cannot be filed.

The chance shown treats every entry as an independent draw that succeeds with the probability below, so n entries give 1 − (1 − p)n. The real chance depends on how many registrations come in at each level, which is unknown before the season. Set the number to match what you expect.

Wages are set per OES area (a metro area or a nonmetropolitan region), so every county in an area shares the same figures. Annual is hourly × 2080 hours. Source: DOL Office of Foreign Labor Certification wage files; county outlines from US Census cartographic boundaries. This is an unofficial reference. Confirm the wage on flag.dol.gov before filing.
